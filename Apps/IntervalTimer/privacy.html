Privacy Policy — Interval Timer

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Interval Timer is published by Massive Joy Soft ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Interval Timer mobile app (the "App") does with your information. In short: the App collects nothing and sends nothing to us — everything it saves stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, store on our servers, sell, or share any personal or sensitive user data. The App has no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking services, and no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or health data.

2. Data stored on your device

The App saves the following on your device only, in its private app storage:

  • Routine settings — work/rest durations, number of passes, rounds, and cue preferences you configure.
  • Presets — routines you save for reuse.
  • App preferences — sound, vibration, appearance, and similar toggles.
  • Session history — completion times and the routine parameters and durations of finished sessions.
  • Planned exercises — titles and settings you queue to do later.

This data is used solely by the App on your device to run the timer and show your history. We have no access to it, and it is not shared with anyone.

3. Export and import

The App never shares your data automatically. It offers optional, user-initiated export (for example, sharing session history as a CSV file or exporting a preset): the file is handed to your device's system share sheet, and you choose if and where it goes. Import works the same way — the App reads only the file you explicitly pick, and processes it on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

4. Permissions the App uses

  • Notifications — to show the current timer phase and its controls while a routine runs. Notifications are created locally by the App; we never send marketing or promotional messages.
  • Vibration — to give haptic cues when a timer phase changes.
  • Foreground service — to keep timing and audio cues accurate while your screen is off, shown with a visible notification.

The App does not request or use access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or personal files.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can clear your session history in the App at any time, and uninstalling the App removes all data it stored. Because we never receive your data, we hold nothing to delete on your behalf — if you have any question about it, contact us using the details below.

6. Children

The App is a general-purpose interval timer and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

7. Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage area, protected by your device's operating system. Keeping your device and its screen lock up to date is the best way to protect the data the App stores.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
